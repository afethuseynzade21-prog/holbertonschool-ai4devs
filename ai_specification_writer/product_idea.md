# Product Idea

## Product Name
CarpoolConnect: Corporate Commute Management Platform

## Vision
Help companies and their employees replace solo commutes with shared rides. CarpoolConnect makes it easy for colleagues to find trusted carpool partners, share commuting costs, and reduce traffic, parking pressure, and carbon emissions. For employers, it turns commuting into a measurable, manageable part of their sustainability and employee-benefits strategy.

## Users

### Primary users
- **Drivers (employees with a car)**: want to fill empty seats, share fuel and parking costs, and arrive at work with predictable schedules.
- **Passengers (employees without a car or who prefer not to drive)**: want an affordable, reliable, and safe way to get to work, ideally with people from the same company.

### Organizational users
- **Mobility / HR / Facility managers**: want to reduce parking demand, offer a commuting benefit, and report on employee commute patterns without handling individual rides.
- **Sustainability / ESG managers**: want reliable data on emissions avoided through carpooling for company reports.

### Key needs
- Trust and safety: ride only with verified colleagues.
- Schedule flexibility: shifts, remote days, and last-minute changes.
- Simple cost sharing without awkward money conversations.
- Privacy: home addresses must not be visible to other users.

## Key Features
- **Company-verified accounts**: sign up with a work email or company single sign-on, so only verified colleagues can join the same network.
- **Smart ride matching**: match drivers and passengers by route, work location, working hours, and flexibility, with recurring weekly commutes.
- **Flexible scheduling**: one-off and recurring rides, remote-day handling, and automatic notifications when plans change.
- **Cost sharing**: transparent per-ride cost split (fuel and parking), with optional integration with a payment provider.
- **Ratings and feedback**: after each ride, participants can rate the experience and report problems.
- **Safety tools**: ride details sharing with a trusted contact, and the option to restrict matches to selected groups (for example, same department).
- **Eco-impact tracking**: estimated CO2 saved per user and per company.
- **Employer dashboard**: aggregated and anonymized statistics (participation, parking spots freed, emissions avoided) with exportable reports.
- **Incentives**: company-defined rewards such as priority parking or points for frequent carpoolers.

## Differentiators
- **Built for companies, not the general public**: matches only within a trusted corporate community, unlike open ride-sharing apps.
- **Employer value, not only employee value**: dashboards and reports that support sustainability goals and reduce parking costs.
- **Privacy by design**: matching uses approximate pickup zones, and exact addresses are shared only after both sides confirm a ride.
- **Commute-focused**: recurring routines and shift schedules are first-class, instead of one-off trips.

## Out of Scope (first version)
- Public ride-sharing outside company networks.
- Built-in navigation (existing map apps are used instead).
- Full payment processing and invoicing (cost split is shown, payment integration comes later).
