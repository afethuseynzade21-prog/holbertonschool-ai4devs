# MVP Concept: EcoRide Office Carpool

## Problem Statement
Many employees drive to the same office alone. This costs them money for fuel and parking, adds traffic and increases CO2 emissions. Colleagues who live in the same area often do not know each other, and arranging shared rides in chat groups is slow, unorganized and hard to trust. There is no simple tool that helps coworkers of one company find and share rides to work.

## Target Users
- **Drivers:** employees who commute by car and have free seats. They want to share fuel costs and reduce commuting stress.
- **Riders:** employees who commute without a car or want a cheaper and greener option. They want to find a reliable ride to the office quickly.

## Core Features
1. **Sign up and log in with a company email.** Only employees of the company can use the app, which builds trust between users.
2. **Post a ride offer.** A driver enters the starting area, departure time and number of free seats.
3. **Search for rides.** A rider searches offers by area and time.
4. **Request a seat.** A rider sends a seat request, and the driver accepts or declines it.
5. **Trip history with estimated CO2 saved.** Each user sees completed shared rides and a simple CO2 estimate.

## Constraints
- **Time and team:** built in a short sprint by one developer with AI assistance.
- **Platform:** web application only, no native mobile app.
- **Scope:** no online payments, no real-time GPS tracking and no map routing. Matching uses area names and times.
- **Users:** the first version is a pilot for employees of a single company.
- **Budget:** free-tier hosting and free or low-cost tools only.
- **Safety:** users are verified only by company email, with no other identity checks in the MVP.
